Data: the list of finals from Wikipedia's yearly WTA 1000 results (CC BY-SA 4.0); match results from Jeff Sackmann's WTA dataset (archive mirror, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: women's singles, from 1990. The tier had between six and ten events a year; the list follows Wikipedia's yearly tables. Charleston was held at Hilton Head until 2000, and Doha and Dubai took turns in the tier in some years. Former events: Tokyo, Boca Raton, Charleston, Berlin, San Diego, Guadalajara, Moscow, Zürich, Chicago and Philadelphia. Several events were cancelled in 2020–2022. Nationality is the one represented at the time (Seles for Yugoslavia until 1993, for example); players who compete as neutrals are shown under their country (Russia, Belarus). Walkovers are left out of match records. Flags by Flagpedia.
