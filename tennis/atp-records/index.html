Data: match results from Jeff Sackmann's ATP dataset (archive mirror, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0); weeks at No. 1 and year-end No. 1s from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: titles count tour-level events (Grand Slams, ATP Finals, Masters, other tour events and the Olympics); team competitions don't count. Match records include the Davis Cup and exclude walkovers. Because titles are counted from match data, a few totals from the 1970s and 80s differ by one from the ATP's own tally (Connors, Lendl, McEnroe). Nationality is the one represented at the time.
