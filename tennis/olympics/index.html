Notes: tennis was played at every Games from 1896 to 1924, then left the programme until 1988 (it was a demonstration sport in 1968 and 1984). In 1908 and 1912 there were indoor and outdoor tournaments; the indoor events are listed as discontinued. In 1896–1904 and in 1988–1992 both losing semi-finalists received a bronze; in 1908–1924 and since 1996 a play-off decides it. In the early Games a doubles pair could join players from two nations; such pairs are shown as a mixed team. Flags by Flagpedia. This project is not affiliated with the ITF or the IOC.
