The career Grand Slam

Data: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0): , plus each tournament's own page for its dates. Dates of birth from Wikidata (CC0). Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: ages are counted on the tournament's last day, when the singles final was played or just after it. Ages at No. 1 use the ranking dates in Wikipedia's lists; for the current No. 1, the latest date in the list. The French Championships count from 1925, when they opened to players from outside French clubs; the 1941–1945 wartime editions aren't counted. Australia held two Australian Opens in 1977 (January and December). The majors go by their current names (the US Open was the U.S. National Championships until 1967). Two early champions whose exact date of birth isn't known, Arthur O'Hara Wood (1914) and Aline Terry (1893), are left out. Flags by Flagpedia.
