Data: match results from Jeff Sackmann's ATP and WTA match files (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0); champions and host cities from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0): . Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: an appearance is an edition in which the player played at least one match, so alternates who came in for a withdrawn player count too. The 1970 and 1971 men's editions were round robins without a final. The men's Masters played in January (1977–1985) count for the previous season, as the ATP lists them. The WTA held two championships in 1986, in March and November, and the 2020 edition was cancelled; the match files start with the 1975 women's edition, so 1972–1974 count only for titles and finals. Earlier Finals formats included 12- and 16-player knockout draws. Flags by Flagpedia.
