Data: match results from Jeff Sackmann's WTA dataset (archive mirror, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0); weeks at No. 1 and year-end No. 1s from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: titles count tour-level events (Grand Slams, the WTA Finals, every tier of the WTA Tour and the Olympics); the Billie Jean King Cup and other team events don't. Match records include the Billie Jean King Cup and exclude walkovers. The match data is incomplete for the 1970s and 80s, so some early totals differ from the WTA's own tally (Evert's 157 titles show as 147, Navratilova's 167 as 169). WTA 1000 counts Tier I (1990–2008), Premier Mandatory and Premier 5 (2009–2020) and WTA 1000 titles. Nationality is the one represented at the time; players who compete as neutrals are shown under their country.
