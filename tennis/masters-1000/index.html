Data: the list of finals from Wikipedia's yearly ATP Masters 1000 results (CC BY-SA 4.0); match results from Jeff Sackmann's ATP dataset (archive mirror, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: men's singles, from 1990. Madrid was played indoors on hard courts in October (2002–2008) before moving to clay in May 2009, when it replaced Hamburg and Shanghai took over the autumn slot. Former events: Hamburg (1990–2008), Stockholm (1990–1994), Essen (1995) and Stuttgart (1996–2001). Canada alternates between Montreal and Toronto. Six events were cancelled in 2020 and Shanghai in 2021 and 2022. Nationality is the one represented at the time; players who compete as neutrals are shown under their country. Walkovers are left out of match records. Flags by Flagpedia.
