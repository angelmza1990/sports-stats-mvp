Uruguayan Derby - Peñarol vs. Nacional

In Uruguay this game is known as "El Clásico" (the Classic, though it is also a derby), and it's one of the most ancient rivalries in the World, even more than Boca Juniors-River Plate from Argentina, Barcelona-Real Madrid from Spain or Milan-Inter from Italy. Both teams are more than hundred years old, Peñarol being founded on 28th September 1891 (*) as CURCC (Central Uruguay Railway Cricket Club) and Nacional on 14th May 1899. Both team dominate the Uruguayan Football scene, with more than 100 League titles and 18 continental/world championships combined. (*) Since the late '1930s, Nacional claims Peñarol is not the same club as CURCC, and that instead it was founded in 1913 (when the name change and independence from the Railway Company occurred). However, there's been no official support to this theory from any neutral organization (and the transition from CURCC to Peñarol in the league from season 1913 to season 1914 was approved unanimously and never contested inside the Uruguayan League).
