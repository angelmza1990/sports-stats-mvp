Spain - Cup 1907

This tournament was played in Madrid for the trophy "Copa del Rey" or "Copa Alfonso XIII". Played as mini-league. 

Participating Clubs:
Club Vizcaya de Bilbao [*]
Madrid FC
Salamanca TFB
Recreativo de Huelva  
Vigo FC

NB: the champions of Catalonia (X Sporting Club) refused to travel to
    Madrid for economical and internal reasons.

[*] Athletic de Bilbao had merged with Unión Vizcaíno to form 
    Club Vizcaya de Bilbao 

Results:

Round 1 [Mar 24]
Vigo FC                 3-3     Recreativo de Huelva
Madrid FC               2-3     Club Vizcaya de Bilbao

Round 2 [Mar 25]
Vigo FC                 1-3     Madrid FC 
Club Vizcaya de Bilbao  5-0     Salamanca TFB

Round 3 [Mar 27]
Salamanca TFB           2-0     Recreativo de Huelva
Vigo FC                 2-1     Club Vizcaya de Bilbao

Round 4 [Mar 28]
Madrid FC               5-0     Salamanca TFB
Club Vizcaya de Bilbao  4-0     Recreativo de Huelva

Round 5 [Mar 29]
Salamanca TFB           1-2     Vigo FC
Madrid FC               4-2     Recreativo de Huelva

   Madrid FC                     4  3  0  1 14- 6  6
 3.Vigo FC                       4  2  1  1  8- 8  5

Because Madrid FC and Club Vizcaya de Bilbao finished equal on points, they
had to play off for the Cup, thereby playing a real final after all.

Final [Mar 30]
Estadio del Hipódromo, Madrid
Madrid FC               1-0     Club Vizcaya de Bilbao
