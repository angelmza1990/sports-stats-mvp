Spain - Cup 1944

Played by 32 teams:
- all 14 teams of First Division
- all 14 teams of Second Division 
- two champions of Third Division (Santander and Mallorca)
- two winners of a six-rounds tournament played by 68 teams of Third Division and 13 
  of Regional categories. They were Gimnástico Tarragona and RCD Córdoba
