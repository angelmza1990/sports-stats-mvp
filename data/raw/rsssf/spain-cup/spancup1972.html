Spain - Cup 1972

COPA DEL GENERALISIMO 1972

Played by 109 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division. Starting at the sixth round (top eight previous season)
       or fourth round (the rest)
 - all 20 teams of Second Division. Starting at the third round
 - 71 (out of 80) of Third Division. Starting at the First Round
