Spain - Cup 1980

COPA DEL REY 1980

Played by 226 teams, the highest number ever:
 - all 18 teams of First Division. 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 39 (out of 40) teams of Second Division B.
 - 148 (out of 160) of Third Division.
 - 1 amateur team (Champion of the Spanish Amateur Championship 1979) 
All teams starting at the First Round, except tohose playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 12 of First Division, 20 teams of Second Division, 39 of Second Division B, 148 teams of 
Third Division and 1 amateur in order to qualify 110 teams. None of the teams of First Div. was eliminated.
Bye: Real Madrid (E1),  Barcelona and Valencia (E2), Sporting Gijón, Atlético Madrid and Real Sociedad (E3)

SECOND ROUND

Played by  Sporting Gijón, Atlético Madrid, Real Sociedad and the 110 winners of the First Round in order 
to qualify 57 teams. None of the teams of First Division was eliminated.
Bye: Real Madrid (E1), Barcelona and Valencia (E2)

FINAL
Played at Santiago Bernabéu (Madrid), June 4th

REAL MADRID CF - CASTILLA CF (MADRID)        6-1
Goals: Juanito (2), Santillana, Del Bosque, Sabido, García Hernández / Alvarez
Real Madrid: García Remón, Sabido, Benito, Camacho, Pirri, Del Bosque, Juanito, Angel, Santillana,
             Stielike (García Hernández), Cunningham (Roberto Martínez)
Castilla: Agustín, Juanito, Herrero, Casimiro, Castañeda, Gallego, Pineda, Alvarez, Paco (Sánchez Lorenzo),
          Bernal, Cidón (Balín)
Referee: Franco Martínez

Castilla CF is the reserve team of Real Madrid, so the final was played 
between two teams of the same club. 
They were the third Second Division team to reach the Final after 
Betis (1931) and Sabadell (1935) and was the first to eliminate 4 teams
of the First Division: Hércules, Athletic, R Sociedad and S Gijón.
