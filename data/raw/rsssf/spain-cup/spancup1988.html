Spain - Cup 1988

COPA DEL REY 1988

Played by 154 teams:
 - all 20 teams of First Division. 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 76 (out of 80) teams of Second Division B. 
    - 17 which were in Second B the previous season 
    - 59 promoted teams which had been promoted from Third AND had finished as top 6 of their groups
 - 38 (out of 348, in 17 groups) of Third Division: 
   - top 6 of each of the 16 groups the previous season (59 were promoted)
   - Mallorca Atlético, relegated from Second B.

First Division teams (except the promoted the previous season) get a bye until the Round of 32.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 118 teams (8 of them received a bye): 4 of Second Division, 76 of Second Division B and 38 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 63 teams.

SECOND ROUND

Played by the 63 winners (7 received a bye) of the First Round in order to qualify 35 teams.
