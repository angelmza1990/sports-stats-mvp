Spain - Cups 1986

COPA DEL REY 1986

Played by 142 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division . 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 26 (out of 40) teams of Second Division B:
            - the 4 relegated from Second Division the previous season
            - top 10 of both 2 groups of Second Division B the previous season (4 of which were promoted)        
            - all 6 teams promoted from Third Division
 - 78 (out of 282) of Third Division: top 6 of each of the 14 groups the previous season (6 were promoted) 
All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 12 of First Division, 20 teams of Second Division, 26 of Second Division B and 78 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 72 teams. Eight teams receveid a bye
Bye: Barcelona (E1), Atlético Madrid (E2), Athletic Bilbao, Real Madrid, Osasuna and Sporting Gijón (E3)
None of the teams of First Division was eliminated.

SECOND ROUND

Played by Sporting Gijón and the 72 winners of the First Round in order to qualify 41 teams. Nine teams received a bye.
Bye: Barcelona (E1), Atlético Madrid (E2), Athletic Bilbao, Real Madrid, Osasuna (E3)
Only one team of First Division was eliminated: Jerez Dep. - Cádiz   1-1, 3-1

COPA DE LA LIGA 1985 - 86

Played by 22 teams:
 - all 18 clubs of First Division. 
 - 3 teams of Second Division: Real Oviedo, Sestao SC and Albacete, as champions of Copa Liga
      of Second Div. and Second Div. B the previous season
 - none of Second Division B
 - 1 team of Third Division: Conquense (Cuenca)

>From Second Round to the Final, a lot of the best Spanish and foreign players couldn't participate, as they were preparing
   World Cup 1986. It have an special effect on Real Madrid and Barcelona (which meet in Second round with a Catalan win 0-4).
   In spite of it, FC Barcelona was champion (only 2 players of the starting list in this final had played the European final 37 days before)
