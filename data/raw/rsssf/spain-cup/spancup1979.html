Spain - Cup 1979

COPA DEL REY 1979

Played by 157 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division. 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 36 (out of 40) teams of Second Division B.
 - 83 (out of 120) of Third Division. 
All teams starting at the First Round, except tohose playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 13 of First Division, 20 teams of Second Division, 36 of Second Division B and 83 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 76 teams.The 13 of First Division won this round.
Bye: Real Madrid (E1),  Barcelona (E2), Athletic Bilbao, Valencia and Sporting Gijón (E3)

SECOND ROUND

Played by Athletic Bilbao and the 76 winners of the First Round in order to qualify 39 teams .
Only 1 team of First Division was sacked: Recreativo Huelva (against AD Ceuta  2-1, 0-3)
Bye: Real Madrid (E1),  Barcelona (E2), Valencia and Sporting Gijón (E3)
