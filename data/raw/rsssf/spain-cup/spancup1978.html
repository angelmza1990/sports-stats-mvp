Spain - Cup 1978

COPA DEL REY 1978

Played by 174 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division. 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 39 (out of 40) teams of Second Division B.
 - 97 (out of 120) of Third Division. 
All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 13 of First Division, 20 teams of Second Division, 39 of Second Division B and 97 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 85 teams.
Only 1 team of First Division was eliminated:  Atlético Baleares - Salamanca   1-1, 1-0
Bye:  Atlético Madrid (E1), Betis (E2), Barcelona, Athletic Bilbao and Las Palmas (E3)

SECOND ROUND

Played by the 85 winners of the First Round in order to qualify 43 teams.
None of the 12 teams of First Division was eliminated
Bye:  Atlético Madrid (E1), Betis (E2), Barcelona, Athletic Bilbao and Las Palmas (E3)
