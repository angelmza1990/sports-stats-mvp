Spain - Cup 1918

Participating Clubs:
Madrid FC (Champions of the region Centro)
Español de Barcelona (Champions of Cataluña)
Recreativo de Huelva (Champions of the region Sur)
Fortuna de Vigo (Champions of Galicia) 
Sporting de Gijón (Champions of Asturias) 
Real Unión de Irún (Champions of Guipúzcoa)          [*]
[*] merger of Irún Sporting and Racing Club de Irún

NB: because of incidents in the championship of the region Norte, the
    Federación Norte was divided into two: Guipuzcoana and Vizcaína.
    Athletic de Bilbao (champions of Vizcaya) declined to participate
    in the Torneo de Copa 1918 out of disagreemen with the regulations.
