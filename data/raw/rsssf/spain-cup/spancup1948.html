Spain - Cup 1948

Played by:
  - all 14 teams of First Division. Bottom six from the fifth round. Top eight from
     the round of 16
  - all 14 teams of Second Division, from the fifth round
  - all 112 teams of Third Division. They play four rounds, so 12 teams pass to the
      fifth. These were: Antequerano, Betis (Sevilla), Imperial (Murcia), C Leonesa (León), 
      Lérida Balompié, Gimn. Lucense (Lugo), Osasuna (Pamplona), Sans (Barcelona), 
      Santander, Toledo, Tomelloso and Atlético Zaragoza.
