Spain - Cup 1991

COPA DEL REY 1991

Played by 211 teams:
 - all 20 clubs of First Division. 
 - 19 (out of 20) clubs of Second Division. Bilbao Athletic couldn't inscribe.
 - 67 (out of 80) clubs of Second Division B. (13 reserve teams couldn't inscribe)
 - 105 (out of 356, in 17 groups) of Third Division: 
   - top seven of each of the 17 groups the previous season (18 had been promoted) (reserve teams excluded)
   - 18 clubs relegated from Second B.

From this season, the "equipos filiales" (Reserve teams) can't inscribe.

Clubs playing European competition get a bye until the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 172 teams (8 of them received a bye): 67 of Second Division B and 105 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 90 teams.

SECOND ROUND

Played by the 90 winners (4 received a bye) of the First Round in order to qualify 47 teams.
