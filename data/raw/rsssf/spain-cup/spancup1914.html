Spain - Cup 1914

The Federación Española de Fútbol took complete control of the cup tournament and decided that the clubs of the existing regional championship would qualify for the national cup championship; no free entry would be permitted anymore. Apart from some exception, the elimination rounds were played in two matches (home and away) from this season onwards. 

Participating Clubs:
Sociedad Gimnástica Española de Madrid
Athletic de Bilbao
España de Barcelona   

Vigo Sporting (champions of Galicia) and Sevilla Balompié (champions of
Andalusia) did not inscribe for the tournament.
