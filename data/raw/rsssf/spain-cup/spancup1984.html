Spain - Cups 1984

COPA DEL REY 1984

Played by 136 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division . 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 26 (out of 40) teams of Second Division B:
            - the 4 relegated from Second Division the previous season
            - top 10 of both 2 groups of Second Division B the previous season (4 of which were promoted). Due to the 
                  extintion of Burgos CF, its place is taken by SD Compostela.
            - all 6 teams promoted from Third Division
 - 72 (out of 260) of Third Division: top 6 of each of the 13 groups the previous season (6 were promoted) 
All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competitions, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 13 of First Division, 20 teams of Second Division, 26 of Second Division B and 72 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 67 teams.
Only one team of First Division was sacked:  Español - Gimnàstic (Tarragona)*   1-0, 1-2 PEN
Bye: Athletic Bilbao (E1), Barcelona (E2), Real Madrid, Atlético Madrid and Sevilla (E3)

COPA DE LA LIGA 1983 - 84

Played by 22 teams:
 - all 18 clubs of First Division. 
 - 1 of Second Division: Atlético Madrileño (reserve team of Atlético Madrid), 
       as champion of CL-Second Division last season
 - 2 of Second Division B: Sporting Atlético (reserve team of Sporting Gijón) and Albacete
       as champions of CL-Second Division, groups I and II, last season
 - 1 of Third Division: Real Madrid Aficionados (second reserve team of Real Madrid)
       as champions of CL-Third Division last season

The best Spanish players didn't play in this Copa Liga, because they were preparing and
  playing European Nations Cup (in which Spain reached the final)
