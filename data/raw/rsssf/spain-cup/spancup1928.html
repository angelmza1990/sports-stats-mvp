Spain - Cup 1928

Played by 26 teams: champions and runners up of the regional championships. But Balears and
Extremadura qualify only one team, which plays in an additional qualifying eliminatory.

Champ. of Catalunya: FC Barcelona, CE Europa (Barcelona)
Champ. of Centro: Athletic de Madrid, Real Madrid
Champ. of Guipúzcoa: Real Unión de Irún, Real Sociedad (San Sebastián)

Champ. of Vizcaya: Athletic de Bilbao, Dep. Alavés (Vitoria)
Champ. of Galicia: RC Deportivo de La Coruña, RC Celta (Vigo)
Champ. of Asturias: Real Oviedo, Racing Langreano
Champ. of Valencia: Levante FC (Valencia), Valencia FC
Champ. of Murcia: Real Murcia, Cartagena FC
Champ. of Andalucía: Real Betis (Sevilla), Sevilla FC
Champ. of Aragón: Iberia SC (Zaragoza), Patria Aragón (Zaragoza)
Champ. of Cantabria: Racing de Santander, RS Gimnástica de Torrelavega
Champ. of Castilla - León: Cultural Leonesa (León), Real Unión de Valladolid
Champ. of Extremadura: Patria (Puebla de la Calzada)
Champ. of Balears: Unió Sportiva Mahón

Real Victoria (Las Palmas), champions of Canary Islands, didnt inscribe.
