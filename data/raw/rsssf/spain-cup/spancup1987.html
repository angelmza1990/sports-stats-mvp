Spain - Cup 1987

COPA DEL REY 1987

Played by 141 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division . 
 - all 18 teams of Second Division.
 - 20 (out of 22) teams of Second Division B. Aragón didn't inscribe. Pontevedra didn't because wasn't initially included a Secon B team.
 - 85 (out of 322) of Third Division: 
             - top 6 of each of the 14 groups the previous season (6 were promoted and automatically qualified,
                   leaving an additional place to the 7th of the group) 
             - Alavés, which had an administrative relegation from Second B
All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 136 teams (10 received a bye): 13 of First Division, 18 teams of Second Division, 20 of Second Division B and 85 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 73 teams.
All the teams of First Division qualified.
Bye: Real Madrid(E1), R Zaragoza (E2), Barcelona, Ahtletic Bilbao and Atlético Madrid (E3)

SECOND ROUND

Played by the 73 winners (7 received a bye) of the First Round in order to qualify 40 teams.
Three teams of First Division were eliminated:
Albacete - Real Murcia                 3-2
Sporting Gijón - Racing Santander*     1-1  PEN
Terrassa - Español(Barcelona)          2-1 
Bye: Real Madrid (E1), R Zaragoza (E2), Barcelona, Ahtletic Bilbao and Atlético Madrid (E3)
