Spain - Cup 1971

COPA DEL GENERALISIMO 1971

Played by 103 teams:
 - all 16 teams of First Division. Starting at the Fifth Round
 - all 20 teams of Second Division. Starting at the Third Round
 - 66 (out of 80) of Third Division. Starting at the First Round
 - the Spanish Amateur champion 1970: "Real Madrid aficionados", starting at First Round
