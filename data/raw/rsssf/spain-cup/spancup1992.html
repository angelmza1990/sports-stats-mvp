Spain - Cup 1992

COPA DEL REY 1992

Played by 207 teams:
 - all 20 clubs of First Division. 
 - 17 (out of 20) clubs of Second Division. Barcelona B, Bilbao Athletic and Real Madrid Dep. couldn't inscribe.
 - 69 (out of 80) clubs of Second Division B. (11 reserve teams couldn't inscribe)
 - 101 (out of 356, in 17 groups) of Third Division: 
   - 14 (out of 18) clubs relegated from Second B. (3 reserve teams excluded; AD Ceuta dissapeared)
   - top six of each of the 17 groups the previous season (18 had been promoted) 
        (if any of them was a reserve team, its place goes to the next in the table)
  
Clubs playing European competition get a bye until the Round of 16.


FIRST  ROUND

Played by 169 teams (9 of them received a bye): 68 of Second Division B and 102 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 89 teams.
Bye: All clubs of First and Second Division, plus Orihuela (lost the place in Second by sanction)

SECOND ROUND

Played by the 89 winners (3 received a bye) of the First Round in order to qualify 46 teams.
Bye: All clubs of First and Second Division, plus Orihuela
