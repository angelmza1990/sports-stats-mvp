Spain - Cups 1985

COPA DEL REY 1985

Played by 142 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division . 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 26 (out of 40) teams of Second Division B:
            - the 4 relegated from Second Division the previous season
            - top 10 of both 2 groups of Second Division B the previous season (4 of which were promoted)        
            - all 6 teams promoted from Third Division
 - 78 (out of 280) of Third Division: top 6 of each of the 14 groups the previous season (6 were promoted) 
All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 12 of First Division, 20 teams of Second Division, 40 of Second Division B and 78 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 71 teams (6 received a bye).
Bye: Athletic Bilbao (E1), Barcelona (E2), Real Madrid, Atlético Madrid, Betis (Sevilla) and Valladolid (E3)
None of the teams of the Primera División was eliminated
SECOND ROUND

Played by Athletic Bilbao, Barcelona, Atlético Madrid, Betis, Valladolid and the 71 winners of the First Round
 in order to qualify 40 teams (4 received a bye)
Bye: Real Madrid (still playing E3)
