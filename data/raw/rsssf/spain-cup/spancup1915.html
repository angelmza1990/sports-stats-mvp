Spain - Cup 1915

Sociedad Gimnástica Española de Madrid were not regional champions and substituted the actual champions Racing de Madrid which could not participate in the cup tournament due to not having inscribed six months in advance at the Federación Española de Fútbol. 

Participating Clubs:
Sociedad Gimnástica Española de Madrid
Athletic de Bilbao
Español de Barcelona  
Fortuna de Vigo

Results:
