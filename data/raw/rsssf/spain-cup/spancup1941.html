Spain - Cup 1941

Played by 34 teams:
 - all 14 teams of First Division (from third round)
 - all 24 teams of Second Division
 - top 6 teams of Third Division
Regional championships are not played yet.

Name changes: from February 1st foreign words are not allowed in the
name of the clubs. So, Sporting or Racing delete this word. Both Athletic 
teams translate their name to Atlético.
