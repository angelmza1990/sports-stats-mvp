Spain - Cup 1934

Played by 28 teams.
As every year, the Regional championships were also the qualifying
round of the Spanish Cup

Qualified teams:

Champ. Centro - Sur (4): Madrid FC, Athletic Madrid, Sevilla FC, Betis (Sevilla)
Champ. Guipuzcoa - Aragón (4): Logroño, Donostia (former Real Sociedad), Zaragoza FC, Osasuna 
Champ. Catalunya (3): Sabadell, Espanyol (Barcelona), FC Barcelona
Champ. Vizcaya (3): Athletic Bilbao, Barakaldo, Arenas (Getxo)
Champ. Galicia (3): Celta (Vigo), Deportivo Coruña, Racing Ferrol

FINAL
Barcelona, May 6th 1934

MADRID FC - VALENCIA FC  2-1

Madrid: Zamora, Ciriaco, Quincoces, Pedro Regueiro, Bonet, Leoncito,
  Lazcano, Luis Regueiro, Samitier, Hilario, Eugenio
Valencia: Cano, Torregaray, Pasarín, Bertolí, Iturraspe, COnde, 
  Torredeflot, Abdón, Vilanova, Costa, Villagrà.
Referee: Vilalta
Goals: 0-1 Vilanova, 1-1 Lazcano, 2-1 Hilario

Note: Madrid FC was the name of current Real Madrid CF in the period 1931-1939.
