Spain - Cup 1947

Played by 28 teams:

- all 14 teams of First Division
- all 14 teams of Second Division


FIRST ROUND
April 20th and 27th

Murcia - Levante UD (Valencia)         1-3, 2-4
Atlético Madrid - Hércules (Alicante)  3-0, 3-4
Baracaldo - Español (Barcelona)        0-2, 0-5
R Oviedo - Zaragoza                    3-1, 1-2
Castellón - Real Sociedad(S.Sebastián) 3-2, 2-3, 3-2*
Ferrol - Real Madrid                   2-1, 2-4
Granada - Celta (Vigo)                 0-1, 2-5
Valencia - Alcoyano (Alcoy)            3-2, 0-1, 2-1**
Deportivo Coruña - R Betis (Sevilla)   1-0, 0-3
Sabadell - RCD Córdoba                 3-0, 0-2
Sevilla - Mallorca                     2-1, 1-1
Gimnástico Tarragona - R Gijón         4-2, 1-1

* Replay at Zaragoza, April 29th
** Replay at Castellón de la Plana, April 29th

Bye: Atlético Bilbao, Barcelona, Santander and CD Málaga

ROUND OF 16
May 4th and 11th

Barcelona - CD Málaga                  3-1, 1-1
Atlético Madrid - Castellón            1-1, 0-3
R Oviedo - Español (Barcelona)         0-0, 1-3
R Betis (Sevilla) - Real Madrid        4-0, 0-6
Valencia - Celta (Vigo)                0-0, 1-1, 0-1*
Atlético Bilbao - Levante (Valencia)   6-2, 2-4
Sabadell - Sevilla                     2-0, 1-2
Santander - Gimnástico Tarragona       6-2, 1-5, 0-1**

* Replay at Madrid, May 14th
** Replay at Barcelona, May 13th

QUARTER - FINALS
May 18th and 25th

Real Madrid - Castellón                2-0, 1-0
Barcelona - Gimnástico Tarragona       0-2, 3-2
Atlético Bilbao - Celta (Vigo)        12-1, 0-1
Sabadell - Español (Barcelona)         2-3, 0-2

SEMIFINALS
June 1st and 8th

Español (Barcelona) - G. Tarragona     5-3, 0-1
Real Madrid - Athletic Bilbao          3-2, 1-0

FINAL
Played at La Coruña, June 22th

REAL MADRID - RCD ESPAÑOL (BARCELONA)        2-0 (aet)
Goals: 1-0 Vidal, 2-0 Pruden  (Both goals at second extra time)
Real Madrid: Bañón, Clemente, Corona, Pont, Ipiña, Huete, Alsúa, Barinaga,
    Pruden, Molowny, Vidal
RCD Español: Trias, Casas, Mariscal, Celma, Fàbregas, Llimós, R. Hernández,
    Jorge, Calvo, Vicente Hernández, Galobart
Referee: Echave

Note that Real Madrid and G Tarragona qualified going up from four goals down in 
   the round of 16, and the 12 goals scored by Atlético Bilbao in quarter-finals,
   a record in matches between First Division clubs.
