Spain - Cup 1981

COPA DEL REY 1981

Played by 138 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division . 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 28 (out of 40) teams of Second Division B:
            - the 4 relegated from Second Division the previous season
            - top 10 of both 2 groups of Second Division B the previous season (4 of which were promoted)        
            - all 8 teams promoted from Third Division
 - 72 (out of 260) of Third Division: top 10 of each of the 8 groups the previous season (8 were promoted) 
All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 12 of First Division, 20 teams of Second Division, 28 of Second Division B and 72 teams of Third Division
 n order to qualify 66 teams.
3 teams of First Division were eliminated:
Logroñés - Osasuna (Pamplona)          4-0, 1-4
R Zaragoza - Endesa Andorra            1-1, 1-2
Las Palmas - CD Tenerife               2-2, 1-2
Bye: Real Madrid (E1), Valencia and Castilla (E2), Real Sociedad, Sporting Gijón and Barcelona (E3)

SECOND ROUND

Played by Valencia, Castilla, Sporting Gijón, Barcelona and the 66 winners of the First Round in order to qualify 35 teams.
4 teams of First Division were eliminated:
Sevilla - R Betis (Sevilla)             2-1, 1-1          
Español (Barcelona) - Terrassa          0-0, 0-1
Palencia CF - R Valladolid              2-1, 1-1
Elche - Real Murcia                     3-0, 0-2
Bye: Real Madrid (E1), and Real Sociedad (E3)
