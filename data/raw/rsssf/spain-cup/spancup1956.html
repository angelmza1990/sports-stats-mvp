Spain - Cup 1956

COPA DEL GENERALISIMO 1956

Played by 16 teams:
- 14 of First Division (R Murcia and Alavés didn't inscribe because they
         had to play relegation play-offs in the same dates)
- two champions of Second Division: Osasuna (Pamplona) and Real Jaén

ROUND OF 16
May 6th and 13th

Deportivo Coruña - Osasuna         1-2, 2-4
Atlético Bilbao - Cultural Leonesa 3-0, 3-2
R Valladolid - Celta (Vigo)        6-0, 1-2
Real Madrid - Real Sociedad        5-0, 2-1
Hércules (Alicante) - Barcelona    1-2, 1-1
Español (Barcelona) - Sevilla      3-2, 0-0
Real Jaén - Valencia               1-0, 1-1
Las Palmas - Atlético Madrid       1-4, 0-6

QUARTER FINALS
May 20th and 27th

Atlético Bilbao - Osasuna          4-1, 2-2
Español (Barcelona) - Barcelona    3-1, 4-4
Real Jaén - Atlético Madrid        2-2, 0-5
Real Madrid - Real Valladolid      4-1, 4-2

SEMIFINALS
June 10th and 17th

Real Madrid - Atlético Bilbao      2-2, 2-3*
Español - Atlético Madrid          1-2, 1-0, 0-3**
* First leg, June 9th
** Replay at Madrid, June 19th

FINAL
Played at Santiago Bernabéu (Madrid), June 24th

ATLETICO BILBAO - ATLETICO MADRID        2-1
Goals: 0-1 Molina, 1-1 Maguregui, 2-1 Arteche
Atlético Bilbao: Carmelo, Orue, Garay, Canito, Mauri, Maguregui,
       Arteche, Marcaida, Arieta I, Uribe, Gainza
Atlético Madrid: Pazos, Martín, Herrera, Verde, Hernández, Cobo,
       Miguel, Peiró, Molina, Agustín, Collar
Referee: Arqué
