Spain - Cup 1982

COPA DEL REY 1982

Played by 136 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division . 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 26 (out of 40) teams of Second Division B:
            - the 4 relegated from Second Division the previous season
            - top 10 of both 2 groups of Second Division B the previous season (4 of which were promoted)        
            - all 6 teams promoted from Third Division
 - 72 (out of 260) of Third Division: top 6 of each of the 13 groups the previous season (6 were promoted) 
All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 13 of First Division, 20 teams of Second Division, 26 of Second Division B and 72 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 66 teams.
1 team of First Division was eliminated: Algeciras - Sevilla          1-1, 2-1
Bye: Real Sociedad (E1), Barcelona (E2), Real Madrid, Atlético Madrid and Valencia (E3)

SECOND ROUND

Played by Real Sociedad, Atlético Madrid the 66 winners of the First Round in order to qualify 34 teams.
Two clubs of Fisrt Division were eliminated:
Racing Santander - Gran Peña (Vigo)*       0-0, 0-0 pen
Cádiz - AD Ceuta                           2-1, 0-2
Bye: Barcelona (E2), Real Madrid and Valencia (E3)
