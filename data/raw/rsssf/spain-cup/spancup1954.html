Spain - Cup 1954

COPA DEL GENERALISIMO 1954

Played by 14 teams:

- top 12 of First Division
- two champions of Second Division: CD Alavés (Vitoria) and UD Las Palmas

ROUND OF 16
May 2nd and 9th

Barcelona - Deportivo La Coruña       4-0, 0-3
Celta (Vigo) - Sevilla                1-1, 0-2
CD Alavés (Vitoria) - Real Madrid     1-2, 0-7
Atlético Bilbao - R Valladolid        3-2, 0-1, 4-1*
Atlético Madrid - Español (Barcelona) 1-3, 1-2
Las Palmas - Real Sociedad            0-0, 1-4

Bye: Valencia and Real Santander

QUARTER FINALS
May 16th and 23th

R Santander - Real Madrid             3-1, 0-3
Barcelona - Atlético Bilbao           4-2, 1-1
Real Sociedad - Valencia              2-5, 1-4
Sevilla - Español (Barcelona)         4-0, 2-6, 2-1*
* Replay at Madrid, May 25th

SEMIFINALS
June 6th and 13th

Real Madrid - Barcelona               1-0, 1-3
Sevilla - Valencia                    0-1, 1-3

FINAL

    Mañó, Fuertes, Badenes, Buqué, Seguí
