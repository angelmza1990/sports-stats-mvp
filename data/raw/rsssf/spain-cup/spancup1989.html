Spain - Cup 1989

COPA DEL REY 1989

Played by 120 teams:
 - all 20 teams of First Division. 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 61 (out of 80) teams of Second Division B. 
    - 4 teams relegated from Second Division the previous season 
    - 57 which were in Second B the previous season 
 - 19 (out of 354) of Third Division: 
   - the 19 teams relegated from Second B.
Note: the 19 clubs promoted from Third Division to Second Division B couldn't inscribe.

20 teams playing in First Division the previous season (including relegated Mallorca and Las Palmas) get a by until the Round of 32.
The other relegated team (Sabadell) was included in Third Round


FIRST  ROUND

Played by 100 teams : 3 of First Division (the newly promoted: Málaga, Elche and R Oviedo),
 17 of Second Division, 61 of Second Division B and 19 teams of Third Division in order to qualify 50 teams.

SECOND ROUND

Played by the 50 winners of the First Round in order to qualify 25 teams.
None of the three First Division teams was eliminated in these two rounds.
