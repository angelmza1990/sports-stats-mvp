Spain - Cup 1949

Played by:
  - all 14 teams of First Division. Bottom six (at January 1st)from the fourth round.
      Top eight from the round of 16.
  - all 14 teams of Second Division, from the fourth round
  - all 84 teams of Third Division play three rounds in order to qualify 12 teams.
       These were: Albacete, Arenas (Guecho), Arenas (Zaragoza), R Avilés, SD Ceuta, 
       RCD Córdoba, Gimn. Lucense (Lugo),Mallorca, Osasuna (Pamplona), Salamanca, 
       Segarra (Vall de Uxó) and Tomelloso.
