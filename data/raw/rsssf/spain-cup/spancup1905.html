Spain - Cup 1905

This tournament was played in Madrid for the trophy "Copa del Rey" or "Copa Alfonso XIII". Played as mini-league. 

Participating Clubs:
Madrid FC
Athletic de Bilbao    
San Sebastián Recreation Club 

NB: the champions of Catalonia were also to play the Cup, but the 
    championship (eventually won by FC Barcelona) was not finished
    in time.
