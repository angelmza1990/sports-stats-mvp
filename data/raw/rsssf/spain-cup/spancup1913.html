Spain - Cups 1913

In 1913 two competitions were played. After tensions between the Federación Española de Fútbol and certain clubs, the championship of the so-called Unión Española de Clubes de Fútbol was played in Barcelona and that of the Federación Española de Fútbol in Madrid.
