Spain - Cups 1983

COPA DEL REY 1983

Played by 136 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division . 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 25 (out of 40) teams of Second Division B:
            - Burgos CF, relegated from Second Division the previous season
            - top 10 of both 2 groups of Second Division B the previous season (4 of which were promoted), except Zamora (See below)       
            - all 9 teams promoted from Third Division
 - 73 (out of 260) of Third Division: 
            - 3 teams relegated from Second Division, whose lost an additional level because of debts (AD Almería, CD Getafe and Levante UD)
            - Zamora CF, which had qualified as top Second B team, but had been relegated for the same reason
            - top 6 of each of the 13 groups the previous season (9 of these had been promoted) 
All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 11 of First Division, 20 teams of Second Division, 25 of Second Division B and 73 teams of 
Third Division in order to qualify 65 teams.
All 11 First Division teams won this round.
Bye: Real Sociedad (E1), Barcelona and Real Madrid (E2), Athletic Bilbao, Valencia, Betis and Sevilla (E3)

SECOND ROUND

Played by Athletic Bilbao, Betis and the 65 winners of the First Round in order to qualify 34 teams.
Two of the 13 First Division teams were eliminated:  Logroñés* - R Zaragoza      1-2, 1-0 PEN
                                                     Erandio - Racing Santander  1-0, 0-0
Bye: Real Sociedad (E1), Barcelona and Real Madrid (E2), Valencia and Sevilla (E3)
