Spain - Cup 1977

COPA DEL REY 1977

With the arrival of the Monarchy, the tournement takes again its former name of Copa del Rey (King's Cup)

Played by 113 teams:
 - all 18 teams of First Division. 
 - all 20 teams of Second Division.
 - 75 (out of 80) of Third Division. 
All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.

FIRST  ROUND

Played by 13 of First Division,20 teams of Second Division and 75 teams of Third Division in order to qualify 54 teams.
Only 1 team of First Division was eliminated: Racing Santander (against Linares CF  2-2,2-2 pp)
Bye: Real Madrid (E1), Atlético Madrid (E2), Barcelona, Español and Athletic Bilbao (E3)

SECOND ROUND

Played by the 54 winners of the First Round in order to qualify 27 teams.
Only 1 team of First Division was eliminated: Burgos CF (against UD Lérida  1-1, 1-1 pp)
Bye: Real Madrid (E1), Atlético Madrid (E2), Barcelona, Español and Athletic Bilbao (E3)
