Spain - Cup 1990

COPA DEL REY 1990

Inscription was reduced, in order to get fixtures for preparations to the World Championship.
Played by 43 teams:
 - all 20 teams of First Division. 
 - 19 (out of 20) teams of Second Division. Bilbao Athletic didn't inscribe.
 - 4 (out of 80) teams of Second Division B: the teams relegated from Second Division the previous season.

All teams starting at the First Round, except those playing European competition, which get a bye until 
they are eliminated of the European Cups, or the Round of 16.
