Spain - Cup 1925

Played by 11 regional champions:

Champions of Catalunya: FC Barcelona
Champions of Centro: Athletic de Madrid
Champions of Guipúzcoa: Real Sociedad (San Sebastián)
Champions of Vizcaya: Athletic de Bilbao
Champions of Galicia: RC Celta (Vigo)
Champions of Asturias: Stadium Ovetense (Oviedo)
Champions of Valencia: Valencia FC
Champions of Andalucía: Sevilla FC
Champions of Aragón: RSA Stadium (Zaragoza)
Champions of Cantabria: Racing de Santander
Champions of Castilla - León: CD Español (Valladolid)

Inscription of Real Murcia FC, champions of new Championship of Murcia, was not accepted.

This year the tournament was played in four groups with a double round-robin
to determine the semifinalists.
