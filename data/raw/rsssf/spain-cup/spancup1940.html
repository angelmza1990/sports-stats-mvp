Spain - Cup 1940

Played by 31 teams. This was the last season in which regional 
championships served as qualifying rounds of the Spanish Cup.

Qualified teams:

Guipúzcoa - Navarra - Aragón (4): Osasuna (Pamplona), Zaragoza, Donostia (current
  Real Sociedad), Alavés (Vitoria)
Catalonia (4): Español (Barcelona), Gerona, Barcelona, San Andrés (Barcelona)
Centro (3): Athletic Aviación (current At.Madrid), Real Madrid, Ferroviaria (Madrid)
Vizcaya (3): Athletic Bilbao, Baracaldo Oriamendi, Erandio
Sur (Andalucía) (3): Sevilla, Betis (Sevilla), Malacitano (Málaga)
Galicia (2): Deportivo La Coruña, Celta (Vigo)
Asturias (3): Sporting Gijón, Sportiva Ovetense (Oviedo), Racing Langreano (Langreo)
Cantabria (2): Racing Santander, Unión Montañesa (Santander)
Valencia (2): Valencia, UD Levante Gimnástico (Valencia, merger of Levante FC and
  Gimnástico)
Murcia (2): Real Murcia, Hércules (Alicante)
Baleares (1): Atheltic Palma
Northern Africa (1): Ceuta Sport
Canary Islands representative (1): Tenerife
