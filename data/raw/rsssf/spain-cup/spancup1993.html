Spain - Cup 1993

COPA DEL REY 1993

Played by 207 teams:
 - all 20 clubs of First Division. 
 - 17 (out of 20) clubs of Second Division. Barcelona B, Athletic B and Real Madrid B couldn't inscribe.
 - 67 (out of 80) clubs of Second Division B. (13 reserve teams couldn't inscribe)
 - 101 (out of 340, in 17 groups) of Third Division: 
   - CD Málaga, relegated from Second A to Third (administrative relegation; actually, disappeared in Aug.1992)
   - 14 (out of 15) clubs relegated from Second B. (Fabril excluded as reserve team of Deportivo)
   - top six of each of the 17 groups the previous season (18 had been promoted) 
        (if any of them was a reserve team, its place goes to the next in the table)
 - 2 of the Regional levels: Fuengirola and Villanovense played in Second B the previous season;
        they were relegated to Third Div; later, had an administrative relegation to the Regional levels.
Clubs playing European competition get a bye until the Round of 16.


FIRST  ROUND

Played by 170 teams (8 of them received a bye): 68 of Second Division B, 101 teams of 
Third Division and 2 of Regional levels, in order to qualify 89 teams.
Bye: All clubs of First and Second Division

SECOND ROUND

Played by the 89 winners (5 received a bye) of the First Round in order to qualify 47 teams.
Bye: All clubs of First and Second Division.
