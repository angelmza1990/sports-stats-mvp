World Series

Every World Series since 1903, when the champions of the American and National Leagues first met: who won, how the titles piled up, which league came out ahead and how long some franchises had to wait.

Every champion

All World Series winners, one tile per Series. The color strip marks the champion's league. Hover or tap a tile for the result and the MVP.

Titles by franchise

All 30 teams, grouped by today's league. Titles won under earlier names and cities count for the current franchise (the Brooklyn Dodgers for Los Angeles, the New York Giants for San Francisco, the Philadelphia and Oakland Athletics for the Athletics).

The longest waits

The longest stretches between two titles, and the waits still running for franchises that have won before. Hatched bars are still open.

The most repeated World Series

Matchups that met more than once with the title on the line, counted by franchise.

So close: most World Series lost

Runner-up finishes by franchise, with every Series that ended in defeat.

How the Series was won

Length of every best-of-seven World Series, from sweeps to Game 7s, split by the champion's league. 1903 and 1919–1921 were best-of-nine; ties (1907, 1912, 1922) are not counted as games won.
