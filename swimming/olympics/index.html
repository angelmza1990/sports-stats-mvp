Data: Wikipedia, (CC BY-SA 4.0). Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: pool events only; the 10 km marathon swim is open water. Nations are shown as they competed (East and West Germany, the United Team of Germany, the Soviet Union, Australasia). Ties are shared medals: when two swimmers tie, both receive the medal and the next one isn't awarded. Swimmers' relay medals follow the team members listed by the source, which for a few early relays leaves out swimmers who only swam the heats. Early Games held events that are no longer swum (yards races in 1904, an obstacle race and underwater swimming in 1900); they are listed under discontinued events. Flags by Flagpedia.
