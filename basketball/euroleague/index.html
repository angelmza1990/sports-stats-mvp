Data: Wikipedia, EuroLeague Championship Game (CC BY-SA 4.0): every final, its awards, the titles by club and the coaches and players with the most titles; every game since 2000–01, for the all-time table, the Elo ratings, home advantage, the rivalries and the clubs' crests, from Euroleague Basketball's public results; the rivalries' totals in every competition from their Wikipedia articles ().

Notes: the competition was the FIBA European Champions Cup until 1991, the FIBA European League until 2000 and the EuroLeague since; in 2000–01 the clubs split between FIBA's SuproLeague and the clubs' own Euroleague, and both champions count, as the source counts them. The 2019–20 season was cancelled because of the COVID-19 pandemic. Clubs are named, and their countries given, as the source's titles table lists them (the Soviet Union and Yugoslavia have no flag here); clubs that never won it go by their name without a sponsor. The all-time table counts every game from the regular season to the final, and its titles column counts every European title since 1958.
