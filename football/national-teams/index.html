Data: Mart Jürisoo's international football results (CC0): every full men's international with its score, competition and venue, and the penalty shoot-outs.

Notes: the head to head counts results after extra time; a match decided on penalties counts as a draw, and the shoot-out winner is noted. Teams of FIFA and of the confederations are included (and historical ones such as East Germany and South Vietnam); matches of regional and unrecognised sides are left out. Teams go by the database's names: Germany includes West Germany and Russia includes the Soviet Union, while Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia and East Germany are teams of their own. The Elo ratings follow eloratings.net's formula (K 60 for the World Cup, 50 for continental championships, 40 for qualifiers and the Nations Leagues, 30 for other tournaments, 20 for friendlies, 100 points of home advantage) but are worked out here from this database: the first twenty teams start at 1500 and every later team at the 25th percentile of the teams already rated, so the numbers differ somewhat from that site's.
