Every Ecuadorian champion since the first national championship in 1957, the clubs of Guayaquil against those of Quito. Great rivalries: Clásico del Astillero (Barcelona SC vs Emelec) and Barcelona vs Liga (Barcelona SC vs LDU Quito). Also see the other leagues: Serie A, La Liga, England, Ligue 1, Bundesliga, Portugal, Netherlands, Scotland, Turkey, Argentina, Brasileirão, Uruguay, Paraguay, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Bolivia, Liga MX and MLS.

Data: Wikipedia, LigaPro Serie A (CC BY-SA 4.0): champions, top scorers and the all-time scorers. Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: no championship was held in 1958 and 1959. 2005 had two championships, the Apertura and the Clausura. The all-time scorers are the five Wikipedia lists, with their goals for every club; no all-time table is published, so this page has none. Every club's title count is checked against the titles-by-club table on the same Wikipedia page. Clubs that no longer exist or have no crest available show their initials.

Club names and crests are trademarks of their respective clubs, shown here for identification only (crests via ESPN's public CDN). This project is not affiliated with LigaPro or the FEF.

Trophies by club: the cups from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0): Copa Ecuador, Supercopa Ecuador; international titles from List of CONMEBOL club competition winners and this site's own data for the world titles. Each cup table is checked against the years it lists, and the international lists against this site's finals.
