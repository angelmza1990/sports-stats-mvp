Notes: titles follow the FIGC's official list, including the 1921–22 season when two federations crowned a champion each (Novese and Pro Vercelli). The 1927 title revoked from Torino and the 1944 wartime championship are not counted. Inter's titles as Ambrosiana-Inter (1929–1945) count for Inter. Every club's title count is checked against the totals table on the same Wikipedia page. Clubs that no longer exist or have no crest available show their initials.
