Data: Wikipedia, List of German football champions (CC BY-SA 4.0); top scorers from List of Bundesliga top scorers by season and Bundesliga, the all-time table from All-time Bundesliga table. Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: before 1963 the title was decided by a final between regional champions; there was no champion in 1904 (the final was not played) and none from 1945 to 1947. East German champions (1950–1991) are not included. Clubs keep the titles of their earlier names (SpVgg Fürth for Greuther Fürth, VfB Leipzig for Lokomotive Leipzig). Every club's title count is checked against the totals table on the same Wikipedia page. Clubs that no longer exist or have no crest available show their initials.
