Notes: the amateur era (1894–1929) follows Wikipedia's list (the USFSA championship and the amateur championships that followed it); the LFP only counts titles from 1932–33. Wartime championships (1939–1945) are not counted. Lille's total includes the two titles of Olympique Lillois, as on the source page. No complete all-time table is published on Wikipedia, so this page has none. Every club's title count is checked against the totals table on the same Wikipedia page.
