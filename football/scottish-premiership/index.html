Data: Wikipedia, List of Scottish football champions (CC BY-SA 4.0): champions and top scorers; the all-time scorers from Scottish Premiership. Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: The title has been the Scottish Football League (1890–1975), the Premier Division (1975–1998), the Scottish Premier League (1998–2013) and the Scottish Premiership since 2013. The first title, in 1890–91, was shared by Dumbarton and Rangers after a 2–2 play-off and counts for both. Every club's title count is checked against the titles-by-club table on the same Wikipedia page. Clubs that no longer exist or have no crest available show their initials.

Club names and crests are trademarks of their respective clubs, shown here for identification only (crests via ESPN's public CDN). This project is not affiliated with the SPFL.

Trophies by club: the cups from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0): Scottish Cup, Scottish League Cup; international titles from List of UEFA club competition winners and this site's own data for the world titles. Each cup table is checked against the years it lists, and the international lists against this site's finals.
