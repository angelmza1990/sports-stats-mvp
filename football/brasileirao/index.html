Notes: the CBF unified the national titles in 2010, adding the Taça Brasil and the Torneio Roberto Gomes Pedrosa, and recognized the 1937 tournament in 2023. In 1967 and 1968 both tournaments were held, so those years have two champions. The 1987 title follows the CBF (Sport Recife). Every club's title count is checked against the totals table on the same Wikipedia page. Clubs that no longer exist or have no crest available show their initials.
