Notes: early tournaments were played by club or amateur selections, and some (1900, 1904) by club sides representing their country. Since 1992 the men's tournament is for under-23 players (since 1996 each squad may add three older players); the women's tournament has no age limit. Some editions were decided by a final round robin instead of a final; the final standings are shown. Nations are shown as they competed (Soviet Union, East Germany, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia). Sections appear only when the source has the data. Flags by Flagpedia. This project is not affiliated with FIFA or the IOC.
