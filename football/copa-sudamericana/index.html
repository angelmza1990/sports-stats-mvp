Notes: “a.e.t.” means after extra time and “pens” a penalty shoot-out. Finals were played over two legs until 2018 and as a single match since 2019. The 2016 final was not played after the crash of LaMia Flight 2933, which killed most of the Chapecoense team; CONMEBOL awarded the title to Chapecoense at Atlético Nacional's request. Wikipedia publishes no all-time table or career scorers for the competition.
