Data: Wikipedia, List of MLS Cup finals (CC BY-SA 4.0); top scorers from MLS Golden Boot and List of Major League Soccer players with 100 or more goals. Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: until 2004 the Golden Boot went to the player with the most points (two per goal and one per assist), not necessarily the most goals. Titles won under earlier names count for today's club (the Kansas City Wizards for Sporting Kansas City, the San Jose Clash for the Earthquakes). No complete all-time table is published on Wikipedia, so this page has none. Every club's title count is checked against the totals table on the same Wikipedia page. Clubs that no longer exist or have no crest available show their initials.
