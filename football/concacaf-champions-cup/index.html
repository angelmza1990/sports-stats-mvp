CONCACAF Champions Cup

The club championship of North America, Central America and the Caribbean since 1962: the Champions' Cup until 2008, the Champions League until 2023 and the Champions Cup since 2024. Also see the Copa Libertadores, the Champions League, Liga MX and MLS.

Data: Wikipedia: list of finals, records and statistics (CC BY-SA 4.0). Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: “a.e.t.” means after extra time and “pens” a penalty shoot-out. Two-legged finals show the score of every leg; some editions were decided in a final group instead of a final. The 1968, 1970, 1973 and 1984 titles were awarded without a final, and in 1978 the final round was not played and three clubs (Leones Negros UdeG, Comunicaciones and Defence Force) were declared joint champions. Every club's title count is checked against the titles-by-club table on the same Wikipedia page. Clubs keep the country they represented; clubs with no crest available show their initials.
