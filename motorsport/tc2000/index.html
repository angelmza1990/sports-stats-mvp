TC2000

Argentina's touring-car championship since 1979: every champion, the drivers, makes and teams that won the most, and every driver who has won a race. The top class was called Súper TC2000 from 2012 to 2021; like the category's own statistics, this page counts it as one championship.

The eras of each make

The champion's make every season. Renault's Fuego won eight titles in a row (1986–1993), Ford and Berta ruled the turn of the century, and Renault returned with the Fluence. Hover or tap a season for the champion and the car.
