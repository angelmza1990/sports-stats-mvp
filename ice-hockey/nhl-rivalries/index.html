NHL Rivalries

Sixteen of the NHL's great rivalries, from the Original Six to the Battle of Alberta and the Battle of Quebec, game by game from the league's own schedules: who leads the head to head, how the lead has moved season by season, every playoff series and the biggest wins.

The lead, season by season

Wins minus losses in every game between them, regular season and playoffs, after each season. Above the line, one side leads the head to head; below it, the other.

Every season

Regular-season wins of each side, season by season: one team's above the line, the other's below. A dot above a season marks a playoff series, in the winner's color, with a ring for the Stanley Cup Final. Hover or tap a season for the details.

Biggest wins

The five widest margins of each side, regular season and playoffs.

Home and away

Wins of each side by where the game was played. In the early decades some games were played at neutral sites.

Data: , every game of the two teams. Updated automatically.

Notes: franchises count with their earlier names (the Toronto Arenas and St. Patricks, the Detroit Cougars and Falcons); the Canadiens–Nordiques rivalry counts the Quebec years only. Ties were part of the game until 2005; since then a game level after overtime is decided by a shootout, and counts as a win for the side that took it. A few early playoff series were decided on total goals. Seasons are named by the years they span.
