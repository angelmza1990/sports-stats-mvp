Notes: covers the Stanley Cup Final era, since 1915; the challenge cups of 1893–1914 aren't included. Until 1926 the Final was played between the NHA (then NHL) champion and the champion of the Pacific Coast and western Canada leagues. No Cup was awarded in 1919 (the Final was cancelled during the flu pandemic) or in 2005 (lockout). Cups are credited to the current franchise; the original Ottawa Senators (1917–1934) are a different club from today's Senators. Logos show each franchise's current identity.
