Matches for the World Championship

Data: , the game files of each player, merged so a game in both files counts once; World Championship matches from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Notes: the files don't hold every game a player ever played, above all the casual and online games of the early years, so the totals are of the games on record. Games are split into classical, rapid and blitz by their event, as on the chess page; Chess960, blindfold, simultaneous and odds games are left out. Exhibition and casual matches with a normal time control count as classical.
