Data: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0): . Head-to-head games from PGN Mentor's player files. Updated automatically twice a year.

Notes: head-to-head records count the games in PGN Mentor's files, which don't record the time control: games are classed as rapid or blitz by their event's name (and, in knockout events, by the game's place in the round; online games count as rapid unless marked blitz). Chess960, blindfold, simultaneous, advanced and computer games are left out. Totals can differ slightly from other databases. Number-one months follow FIDE's lists as published: an unchanged month keeps the previous number one, and FIDE left Kasparov off its 1994 lists after the PCA split, so those months go to Karpov. Rapid and blitz rating lists began in 2014 in this record. Federations are shown as they were (Soviet Union, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia); players who competed under the FIDE flag are shown as neutral. Flags by Flagpedia.
