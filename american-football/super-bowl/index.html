Notes: titles are credited to the current franchise (the Baltimore Colts count for the Colts, the Oakland Raiders for the Raiders, the Houston Oilers for the Titans). NFL championships follow the league's own count: the NFL title (decided by the standings until 1932 and by a championship game from 1933) through the 1969 season, then the Super Bowl. AFL titles (1960–1969) aren't included. Years are those of each Super Bowl, played early in the following calendar year; league titles are listed by season.
