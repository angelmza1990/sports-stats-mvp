NFL Rivalries

Seventeen of the NFL's great rivalries, from Bears–Packers, played since 1921, to the NFC East, the AFC West and the Ravens and the Steelers: who leads the head to head, how the lead has moved season by season, every playoff meeting and the biggest wins, game by game.

The lead, season by season

Wins minus losses in every game between them, regular season and playoffs, after each season. Above the line, one side leads the head to head; below it, the other.

Every season

Regular-season wins of each side, season by season: one team's above the line, the other's below. A dot above a season marks a playoff game, in the winner's color, with a ring for the Super Bowl. Hover or tap a season for the details.

Home and away
